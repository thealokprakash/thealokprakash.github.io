Research directions

Questions worth investigating.

My research interests span material behaviour, joining processes, computational analysis, and engineering applications.

🔩

Welding Metallurgy & Dissimilar Joining

TIG and P-TIG welding, Ni-based superalloys, stainless steels, interlayer effects, and weld microstructure–property relationships.

🔬

Materials Characterisation

Optical microscopy, SEM-EDS, EBSD, tensile and impact testing, microhardness, heat treatment, and corrosion testing.

🧮

Computational Mechanics

Finite element analysis, numerical simulation, statistical methods, material selection, and predictive engineering frameworks.

🏭

Advanced Manufacturing

Process development, design for manufacturing, jig and fixture design, and manufacturing optimisation.

⚡

Electric Mobility

EV product development, chassis engineering, component validation, vehicle reliability, and thermal testing.

📈

Data-Driven Engineering

Scientific computing and predictive analysis for engineering problems. Predictive analysis of interlayer effects is a proposed framework, not a completed validated result.
