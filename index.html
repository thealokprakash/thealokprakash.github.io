About Me

I am a mechanical engineer with an M.Tech in Design and Manufacturing from the National Institute of Technology Silchar. My academic work focused on dissimilar joining of a Ni-based superalloy and austenitic stainless steel using TIG welding with a Ni interlayer.

My interests include welding metallurgy, materials characterisation, microstructure–property relationships, computational mechanics, advanced manufacturing, and data-driven engineering. In industry, I have worked on electric vehicle development, thermal validation, materials qualification, component testing, root-cause analysis, and manufacturing improvement.

I am interested in applying rigorous experimental and computational methods to engineering problems, especially those involving materials, structural performance, manufacturing processes, and sustainable mobility.
