About Alok

Mechanical engineer with a research foundation in materials and manufacturing, and hands-on experience developing electric vehicles.

Academic background

Design, materials & manufacturing

I completed an M.Tech in Design and Manufacturing at the National Institute of Technology Silchar. My thesis examined dissimilar joining of a Ni-based superalloy and austenitic stainless steel using TIG welding with a Ni interlayer.

My interests include welding metallurgy, material characterisation, microstructure–property relationships, computational mechanics, advanced manufacturing, and data-driven engineering.
