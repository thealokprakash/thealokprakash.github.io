BACKGROUND

My journey

I am a mechanical engineer with a postgraduate specialisation in Design and Manufacturing from the National Institute of Technology Silchar. My interests span mechanical design, materials engineering, manufacturing processes, and product development.

My professional experience includes electric vehicle development, vehicle subsystem integration, component validation, manufacturing coordination, and engineering problem-solving. I have worked across the product lifecycle, from concept development and design decisions to testing, troubleshooting, and production support.

My academic research focused on dissimilar-metal welding of Inconel 718 and AISI 321 stainless steel using a nickel interlayer. This work involved welding-process development, microstructural characterisation, and mechanical-property evaluation.

I enjoy working on problems that require both analytical thinking and practical implementation, particularly where material behaviour, design decisions, manufacturing constraints, and product performance intersect.
